Guzheng

Nine years.

Claire has studied guzheng for nine years. Under the guidance of Chen Wenying, her practice has developed from foundational technique to interpretation and the performance of complete works.

Behind the performance

From passages
to a whole evening.

Preparing a full solo recital involves more than refining each work. It also means attending to the transitions between pieces, changes of mood, and the concentration needed to sustain the programme.

What I am listening for

Draft reflection · Claire will revise this in her own words.

I want a phrase to feel intentional, not simply correct. I am learning to hear the difference between repeating a passage and understanding what I want it to say. That changes where I place my attention when I practise.
