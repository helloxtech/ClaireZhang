Claire Zhang

Student artist and Guzheng performer

I am a student at Semiahmoo Secondary School in Surrey, British Columbia. Through painting and Guzheng, I explore observation, patience, and the ways creative work can connect people across generations and communities.

Visual Art

Featured Work

A selection of recent paintings and drawings exploring light, observation, and quiet attention.

Oil on Canvas · 2025

Afternoon Light, Studio Table

A study of how warm light transforms familiar objects. I wanted to capture the way shadows shift colour when they land on linen.

Watercolour on Paper · 2025

Morning Mist, Boundary Bay

This painting grew from a morning walk. I was interested in how fog simplifies a landscape into just a few tonal layers.

Charcoal on Paper · 2024

Quiet Reflection

Drawing someone older teaches patience. Every line in a face carries time, and I wanted the charcoal to hold that weight.

Music

Featured Performance

▶

Erta Mektige (Lilac Vine Flower) — High Mountains and Flowing Water

Traditional Chinese Community Cultural Night 2025 Solo Performance

This piece is one of the most well-known works in the Guzheng repertoire. Performing it at a community event felt meaningful because it bridges generations — many in the audience grew up hearing this music, and sharing it helps keep that connection alive.

Community

Sharing Work Beyond the Studio

Alongside independent practice, I have been fortunate to share Guzheng music at community events and to keep learning how performance changes when it is meant for real people in real spaces.

2025

Lunar New Year Cultural Celebration

Surrey Chinese Cultural Association · Solo Performer

Performed a traditional Guzheng piece for an audience of over 200 community members celebrating the Lunar New Year.

2024

Senior Centre Music Visit

Crescent Gardens Senior Living · Volunteer Performer

Shared a short recital of classical Chinese pieces with elderly residents. Several audience members told me the music reminded them of home.

2024

Semiahmoo Arts Night

Semiahmoo Secondary School · Solo Performer

2023

Multicultural Heritage Festival

City of Surrey · Youth Performer

Selected to represent Chinese musical traditions at the annual heritage festival. Performed two pieces for a diverse outdoor audience.
