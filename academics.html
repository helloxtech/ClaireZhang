Questions & methods

Questions I want
to keep following.

I am interested in the places where making, listening, and the technology meet. These are possible directions for the next stage of my learning.

Exploratory copy · The intended study direction spans art, design, and creative technology. These questions are proposed language for Claire to revise, not claims of completed research or technical expertise.

01 · Visual culture

What do images
help us remember?

I want to look more carefully at what an image makes visible, what it leaves outside the frame, and how those choices shape the stories we tell about a place.

02 · Music & listening

What travels
with a tradition?

Learning a traditional instrument raises questions about continuity and change. What do we carry forward when we perform, and what do we discover in a new setting?

03 · Interaction

What makes an interaction
worth someone’s attention?

A touch, a delay, or a change in rhythm can change what someone notices. I want to explore how visual feedback and physical gestures can make a digital experience feel more understandable and personal.

04 · Learning through making

What can a revision
teach us?

A process can reveal something that a finished work cannot. I want to become better at noticing how a decision changes, and at explaining why.

I want an education where I can move between the studio and the prototype: learning to make images, question systems, and create experiences that invite other people in.
