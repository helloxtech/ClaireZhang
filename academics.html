Notes

Questions worth
staying with.

A growing set of questions at the meeting point of visual art, sound, and interaction.

01 · Looking

What does an image ask us to notice?

How do framing, colour, repetition, and absence change the way a place or moment is understood?

02 · Listening

What travels with a tradition?

What can sustained study of an instrument reveal about continuity, interpretation, and bringing a practice into a new setting?

03 · Interaction

When does a response feel meaningful?

How might touch, time, and visual feedback make an experience more attentive rather than merely faster?

04 · Process

What can revision make visible?

How can sketches, tests, and changed decisions show the thinking that a finished image conceals?

The aim is to learn through making: to observe closely, test an idea, and explain what changed.
