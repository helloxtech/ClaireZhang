Study interests

Art, design
& creative technology.

Nine years of guzheng bring questions of listening, rhythm, timing, and gesture into Claire’s intended undergraduate direction. She plans to explore them through visual art, interaction, and creative technology.

01 · Visual rhythm

Art & design

How can repetition, spacing, colour, and material guide attention in an image?

A future studio study might translate the pulse of a phrase into pencil, ink, cut paper, or a sequence of marks—then notice what changes when repetition tightens, breaks, or leaves space.

02 · Gesture & response

Interaction

How might a visitor’s movement change what they see or hear?

A future interactive study could map a touch, sweep, or pause to the intensity of an image or sound. The question is how feedback can remain legible and attentive rather than merely reactive.

03 · Sound as material

Creative technology

What might it mean to see or participate in a digital sound experience?

A future digital study could let sound leave a changing visual trace as a viewer moves or pauses. It would test how timing and participation alter a shared experience.

A musical starting point

Claire’s guzheng study is the musical starting point for these questions about rhythm, gesture, listening, and participation.
