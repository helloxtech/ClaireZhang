# Guzheng study sample

- Source: [GUZHENG - instrument- Single Note - Sound](https://freesound.org/people/nanliu_music/sounds/847157/) by **nanliu_music**, Freesound #847157, published March 4, 2026.
- License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), verified on the source page October 23, 2026. Attribution is provided voluntarily in the demo.
- Public source preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/847/847157_18537710-hq.mp3
- The uploader describes an acoustic guzheng recording. It is not a recording by Claire. We have not independently verified its recording session.
- Local derivative `guzheng-a3.mp3`: first 5.5 seconds only (the source contains another attack later), converted to mono 44.1 kHz/160 kbps MP3, 45 Hz high-pass, 0.7 gain, final 0.7-second fade. No generated performance or musician attribution.
- The measured fundamental of the first attack is approximately 218.6 Hz (near A3). The browser resamples this single note to D3/E3/F♯3/A3/B3, with a continuous upward bend of up to two semitones. These are creative transformations, not a physical guzheng simulation or a recording of a performed phrase.
- `listening.js` adds a quiet generated convolution tail. Audio is local, opt-in, and never recorded or transmitted.
