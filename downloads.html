Preparing the real work

Keep the thinking
with the image.

For each selected work, gather its title, date, medium, dimensions, and Claire’s own role. Choose a few process images that show a decision changing—not just the steps of execution.

For an interaction, add its source idea, a storyboard or early experiment, and a short demonstration of what someone actually does. Credit collaborators and tools, including any AI assistance.
