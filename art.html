Work & ideas

Look closely.
Make a connection.

Visual art, material exploration, and interaction can begin with the same question: what happens when we pay closer attention? These proposed studies show how a question could become a body of work.

Portfolio planning draft · The studies below are proposed directions, not completed works or an existing body of art. Claire’s original artwork and process images are still needed. Start with existing work; these are possibilities, not a requirement to make three new projects.

  1. 01
    Proposed study

    Light at
    the threshold.

    How does a familiar place change when the light changes?

    Medium or interaction

    Drawing & painting from observation. Return to one ordinary interior at different times of day. Compare colour, shadow, and empty space through a small series of drawings or paintings.

    Evidence to add

    An original observational drawing, an early colour study, and a final work. Include actual media, dimensions, dates, and a brief note about what changed.

    What Claire will explain

    Which decision changed the feeling of the place? What did returning to the same subject reveal that the first study missed?

  2. 02
    Proposed study

    Hands that
    remember.

    Can a drawing make years of practice visible?

    Medium or interaction

    Gesture, drawing & material exploration. Use the gestures of tuning, playing, and resting as a starting point. Explore marks, repeated forms, or layered materials to suggest the physical memory of practice.

    Evidence to add

    Claire’s own reference photographs, observational sketches, material trials, and one resolved piece. Distinguish reference material from original artwork.

    What Claire will explain

    What makes a gesture feel practised or personal? Which material choice conveys that, and which experiment did not work?

  3. 03
    Proposed study

    A listening
    map.

    How might someone participate in a sound through an image?

    Medium or interaction

    Visual system & interaction prototype. Begin with an original recording or a rhythm Claire knows well. Sketch a visual response to one action: a touch, a pause, or a change of pace. A paper sequence can come before a digital prototype.

    Evidence to add

    The source rhythm, visual experiments, a storyboard, and—if Claire develops it—a short recording of the interaction. Credit every collaborator, tool, and borrowed asset.

    What Claire will explain

    What does a visitor do, and what changes in response? How does that response connect to the musical idea? What changed after trying it with someone?

The finished portfolio

Original work.
Visible thinking.

Each selected work will include its actual title, year, medium, dimensions, and context. A few well-chosen process images and Claire’s own account of a decision will make the work easier to understand.

The final selection should come from work Claire can discuss confidently and in detail. For collaborative or digital work, state her own contribution and the tools used, including any AI assistance.
