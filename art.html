Keyboard: focus a string, then 1–5 to pluck, ↑/↓ to bend, Esc to quiet. Enter or Space also plucks.

How this feature works

One gesture.
Sound and image.

A touch triggers a guzheng sample. Moving across the strings creates a sequence of notes; moving upward while holding bends the pitch. The same gesture changes the shape of the visual trace, while measured audio energy changes the image’s intensity.

The controls offer a small illustration of gesture-based interaction. They do not simulate the full technique or physical behaviour of a guzheng.

The backdrop was generated with OpenAI’s image-generation tool for this website. It is decorative imagery, not artwork by Claire. The sample is a third-party recording credited above.
