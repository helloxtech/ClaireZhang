Work & ideas

A question,
taking shape.

Three possible directions, beginning with looking or listening. The AI-generated images show a visual direction; they are not completed student work.

01 / Proposed direction

Light at the threshold

How does a familiar place change when the light changes?

One room, seen again and again. A shift in colour or an empty patch of wall could turn an ordinary space into something unfamiliar.

Possible form
A sequence of observational drawings and paintings made in one interior, at different times of day.
If developed
An original observation, a later revision, and the decision that changed the image.
Next direction
02 / Proposed direction

Hands that remember

Can a drawing make years of practice visible?

Some knowledge lives in a gesture. This direction would explore how repeated marks and layered materials could make the memory of practice visible.

Possible form
Drawings and material experiments based on gestures of tuning, playing, and resting.
If developed
Close observation, material experiments, and a resolved work with a note on what changed.
Next direction
03 / Proposed direction

A listening map

How might someone participate in sound through an image?

What if listening left a visible trace? A rhythm could become a visual response, giving a visitor a way to explore a musical idea through a simple gesture.

Possible form
A visual system that responds to one action: a touch, a pause, or a change of pace. A paper sequence can come before a digital prototype.
If developed
A source rhythm, visual experiments, and any working interaction Claire actually develops.
