Light at the threshold

What does repetition teach the eye?

In music, returning to a passage can reveal a detail missed the first time. This visual study carries that habit into looking: one room, revisited as daylight moves.

The experiment
A cobalt threshold meets a coral interior. The image isolates light and shadow so that a familiar room can be read as intervals of colour.
The artistic decision
Large colour fields carry the rhythm; small architectural edges hold the composition. Selective detail leaves room for the eye to complete the space.
A question to test
Does returning change the image—or change what the observer notices?

Hands that remember

What can a hand show before a sound?

Claire’s nine years of guzheng study give this visual direction a specific starting point: the hands. Hovering, plucking, pressing, and releasing become a visual vocabulary of marks and materials.

The experiment
An AI-assisted collage places hands, strings, and layered paper in one frame. Repetition turns a moment of contact into a visible rhythm.
The artistic decision
The hand supplies a point of focus; the repeated lines suggest continuity beyond it. The image explores the interval between tension and release.
A question to test
Can someone who has never played guzheng read a difference between tension and release?

A listening map

Can a pause become something visible?

Guzheng practice offers a starting question about timing: what happens when a gesture becomes a sound, and a sound becomes a mark? This website study connects plucking, bending, and listening to an image that changes with the visitor.

Visitor action
Pluck one string, sweep across several, or hold and slide upward to bend a note. Leave space to hear it settle.
Visual response
Each note leaves a luminous trace. A bend curves it; a sweep layers several traces. The image quiets as the sound fades.
A question to test
Can the image help a visitor hear the difference between a single touch, a continuous gesture, and a pause? The instrument below invites that comparison.
