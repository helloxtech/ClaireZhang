Selected directions

Three ways of
paying attention.

These illustrated directions frame Claire’s intended study interests. They are concept imagery for this website, not Claire’s submitted or completed work.

01 / Visual rhythm

Light at the threshold.

How can a change in light make an ordinary place feel newly seen?

A future studio direction could use observation, repetition, and material to trace the distance between a room’s structure and its atmosphere. It might begin with drawing, paint, paper, or a sequence of images.

02 / Gesture & response

Hands that remember.

How might a familiar gesture become visible, responsive, or shared?

Guzheng study brings attention to touch, timing, and repetition. A future interaction direction could map a sweep, pause, or pressure to changes in image and sound, asking when feedback feels attentive rather than automatic.

03 / Sound as material

A listening map.

What might it mean to see or participate in a digital sound experience?

A future creative-technology direction could let sound leave a changing visual trace as a visitor moves or pauses. The focus is on timing, participation, and the visual shape of listening.
