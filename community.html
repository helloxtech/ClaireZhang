Why share it? · Draft

Making space
for listening.

I hope that a listener does not need to know the instrument already to find a way into the music. A change in texture, a familiar feeling, or a moment of quiet can be a starting point.

Preparing a programme asks me to think beyond individual passages. I am learning to pay attention to the contrasts between works and the concentration it takes to stay present from beginning to end.

After the recital

What actually
happened matters.

This space is reserved for a short, specific reflection after the performance: one moment Claire remembers, what she heard differently, and what she wants to work on next.

Actual performance photographs or recordings can accompany that reflection. Other community activities will be added when their dates, Claire’s role, and supporting material are confirmed.
