Canada YC Music Academy announces Claire Zhang as soloist for a graduation guzheng recital on October 25, 2026 in Richmond. The announcement identifies Chen Wenying as her teacher.

Why it matters

Making space
for listening.

Performance changes the scale of practice. It asks a performer to shape a sequence of works, hold attention across transitions, and offer an audience a way into an instrument they may not already know.

That public dimension connects music to Claire’s interest in art and interaction: how a considered experience can invite someone to pause, look, or listen more closely.
