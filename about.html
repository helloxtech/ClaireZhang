A student exploring visual art, interaction, and sound—with a sustained practice in guzheng.

Personal statement draft · This is a proposed voice and direction for Claire to make her own.

I am still learning what kind of artist I want to become.

A practice built over time

Attention before
easy answers.

I am drawn to practices that reward patience: listening closely, returning to a difficult passage, and noticing what changes when I look again.

In visual art, I want to understand the choices behind an image. In music, I am learning how an attack, a pause, or a release can alter a whole phrase. Both make me more willing to revise my first idea.

Claire’s nine years of guzheng study and her work under Chen Wenying are documented in the Academy’s recital announcement. The programme announced for October 25, 2026 is a complete solo recital at Canada YC Music Academy in Richmond.

Looking ahead · Draft

Room to make.
Room to question.

I want to study where art, design, and technology meet: to draw from observation, think through materials, and discover what happens when someone can participate in a work rather than only look at it.

I do not need every answer yet. I want to become better at noticing what deserves a closer look, and at staying with it long enough to learn something.
