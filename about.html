About

Claire Zhang

A student exploring visual art, interaction, and sound—rooted in a sustained practice of guzheng.

A practice built over time

Attention before
easy answers.

Claire is drawn to practices that reward patience: listening closely, returning to a difficult passage, and noticing what changes when she looks again.

Her interest in visual art and interaction begins with attention to an image, a material, a gesture, or a pause. Music offers one way to understand how a small change can reshape a whole phrase.

About this site

Experience,
site studies, and sources.

Claire’s documented experience on this site is her guzheng study and the solo graduation recital announced by the Canada YC Music Academy. The announcement identifies nine years of study, names Chen Wenying as her teacher, and lists the 25 October 2026 recital. The portrait comes from Claire’s original site; the recital poster comes from the Academy’s public announcement.

The illustrated studies on Home and Work are AI-assisted visual directions made for this website. They are not Claire’s artwork or a record of her process. The interactive listening instrument is also a site-made, AI-assisted study. It uses a credited CC0 guzheng sample processed in the browser; the recording is not Claire’s.

The site brings these distinct sources together to show a possible connection between Claire’s sustained music practice and her interest in visual art, interaction, and sound.
