21-string form · five-note studyGuzheng · gesture · light
A space waiting for your touch.D pentatonic
  1. 01 / PluckTouch one of five highlighted sound zones on the 21-string form. A note becomes a line of light.
  2. 02 / Bend & sweepHold and move sideways to bend; sweep up or down across the five sound zones to play a phrase.
  3. 03 / ListenLift your hand. Let the sound and its trace settle.

Sound is off. Enable it when you are ready; you can explore the image silently.

Keyboard: focus a string, then 1–5 to pluck, ←/→ or ↑/↓ to bend, Esc to quiet. Enter or Space also plucks.
